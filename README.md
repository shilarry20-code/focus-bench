# Focus Desk

Focus Desk is a study timer that turns studying into a game you play alongside a world of simulated students. You run focus sessions (Pomodoro, Flowtime, custom timers and more), and your real study time feeds everything else. You can play ranked 1v1 and 4/8/16-player group matches where whoever studies longest wins, join casual lobbies, and watch live top matches or replays of past ones. There's an Instagram-style feed with posts and stories, Discord-style DMs and group chats where people reply through Gemini, and leaderboards, streaks, levels and achievements. It also has study guides with quizzes and flashcards. The simulated world is generated from the clock, so it's the same on every device. Your own data stays in your browser, with optional sync through a GitHub gist.

Open `index.html` or serve the folder; there's no build step. It's hosted on GitHub Pages.
